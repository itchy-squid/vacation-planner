Getting started on the Ideas board

Someone new opens a trip, lands on Ideas, and sees a trip name, “Ideation · 0 pins”, and a small + in the corner. Nothing tells them what an idea is, how to add one, or that Ideas feeds Plan and Itinerary. These mockups show five ways to fix that. They build on each other, and the recommended order is at the bottom. The phones are clickable.

Today

What a new trip looks like now

The only action on the screen is a 30px “+” in the header, next to the settings gear. There are no region chips because there are no pins, so most of the screen is blank.

  • Creating a trip sends you back to Trips Home, not into the trip. You then have to choose between “Open board” and “Start schedule” before you know the difference.
  • “Pins”, “Ideation” and “board” are three names for one thing. The tab says “Ideas”.
  • Plan opens on an empty day grid with “0 unplaced”. It doesn’t say that ideas come from the other tab.

Concept 1

An empty board that explains itself Recommended

When a trip has no ideas, the board says what it is for in one sentence and offers the three ways to start as large, labeled rows. Below them, two faded example cards show what an idea looks like once it’s added, with a photo, region, cost and who added it.

  • “Paste a link” and “Type a place” both open the existing New pin form, with the right field focused.
  • “Invite people” opens the invite sheet, so the owner learns early that others can add ideas too.
  • Readers see a different message: “Nothing here yet. Amanda is still collecting ideas.” No actions are shown.
  • The example cards go away as soon as the first real idea is added.
Effort: small · PinBoard.jsx only · no backend change

Concept 2

Add an idea without leaving the board Recommended

Today adding an idea is a full-screen form with five fields, then the edit screen. A bar above the tabs takes a link or a name, adds the card right away, and keeps you on the board so you can add several in a row. The card shows “Add time and cost” so people know there’s more they can fill in later.

  • A link fills in the title from the page, the same way deriveTitleFromLink does now.
  • After adding, a short message offers “Add details” to open the edit screen.
  • Suggestion chips appear only when the board is empty, to show the kind of thing people add.
  • The “+” in the header can stay for people who want the full form.
Effort: small to medium · new QuickAdd component · uses the existing CREATE_PIN
Try it: type a place and press Add.

Concept 3

Show where the trip is, and what comes next

A thin four-step bar under the trip name: Ideas, Plan, Decide, Itinerary. It fills in from the trip’s real state, using data the app already has: the number of ideas, placed blocks, open votes and locked days. Below it, one card says what to do next. It changes as the trip moves along and can be dismissed.

  • 0 ideas: “Add a few ideas to get started.”
  • 3 or more ideas and nothing placed: “Ready to plan? Put your ideas onto days.” with a button to Plan.
  • Open votes: “2 blocks are waiting for your vote.”
  • This works on every main screen, not only Ideas. It answers “what is this tab for?”
Effort: medium · a new TripProgress component in TripHeader · reads PlannerContext
Try it: tap a step to see its nudge.

Concept 4

Land in the trip with a short checklist

After “Create trip”, open the new trip’s Ideas board instead of going back to Trips Home. A sheet lists the four things that make a trip useful. Each item links to where it gets done and ticks itself off when that happens. You can close the sheet, and it becomes a “2 of 4” pill in the header until everything is done.

  • Items: name the trip (already done), add your first idea, invite travelers, put an idea on a day.
  • The checklist is stored per member, so people who join by invite get a shorter version: add an idea, look at Plan.
  • The redirect is a one-line change in NewTrip.jsx. It helps even if the checklist ships later.
Effort: medium · a per-member “dismissed” flag or localStorage · redirect is trivial
Try it: tap the items to check them off.

Concept 5

Connect Ideas and Plan

The two tabs don’t mention each other. Small changes on both sides make the path between them clear.

  • Plan with no ideas says “Ideas you add show up here to place on days” and offers a button to Ideas, instead of an empty grid and a disabled “0 unplaced”.
  • A count on the Plan tab shows how many ideas aren’t on a day yet, so there’s a reason to open it.
  • “Add to a day” on each idea (in the edit screen or as a long-press on the card) opens Plan with that idea ready to place.
Effort: small for the empty state and badge · medium for “Add to a day”

Suggested order

Ship in three small steps

  1. Land in the trip, and explain the empty board. Change NewTrip.jsx to go to /trips/:id/board after creating a trip, and add Concept 1’s empty state, including the reader version. This is the cheapest fix for the exact problem.
  2. Quick add, and connect the tabs. Add Concept 2’s bar and Concept 5’s Plan empty state and tab count. Together they make “add ideas, then place them” clear without any tutorial.
  3. Guidance over time. Add Concept 3’s next-step card, or Concept 4’s checklist. Both answer “what now?”, so pick one: the checklist suits first-time setup, and the progress bar stays useful for the rest of the trip.

Either way, use one word everywhere. The tab says “Ideas”, so the screen caption, the “+” label, the New pin form and Trips Home (“Open board”, “pins”) should all say “idea” too. Each step needs an e2e test for the new-user path: create a trip, see the empty board, add an idea, and see it on Plan.
