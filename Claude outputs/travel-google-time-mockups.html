Google's time for travel, and travel between anything

Two changes to the travel you add on a day. The Travel form now asks Google how long the trip takes and fills in the minutes for you, even when you type the places instead of picking ideas on the map. And the + travel button now sits between every two activities, including ones back to back, showing Google's drive time. When the drive doesn't fit, adding it pushes what's after it later.

On the day

A + travel button between every two activities

Before, the chip only showed in a gap of 20 minutes or more. Now it shows between any two blocks for everyone, centred on the gap or on the seam between back-to-back blocks. When both ends are ideas on the map, it shows Google's drive time. It turns rust when the drive is longer than the gap.

Rental car pickup ends at 3:15 PM and check-in starts at 3:15 PM. The 35-minute drive doesn't fit, so the chip is rust. The 14-minute drive to Disney Springs fits in the 75-minute gap.

How it behaves

  • Google is asked for the mode you picked, once you've filled in From and To. Ideas on the map use their spot. Typed text is searched near the trip's places.
  • Google's minutes fill the field until you type your own. Clearing the field hands it back to Google.
  • Bus and train are timed for that trip day's weekday, at the leave time, or 10:00 AM if there's none. Drive and walk ignore the time.
  • Answers are remembered for the page, so the chip's drive time and the form's don't cost two requests.

Pushing later blocks

  • Only from a + travel chip. Each block in the way moves just past the one before it, and the push stops at the first gap big enough to absorb it.
  • Votes, locked blocks, and blocks that run past midnight aren't moved. The form says why, and saving falls back to "tap a free time to place it".
  • No chips on a day split into groups, as before.
