Day notes, and telling pinned ideas apart

Two asks. Notes on a calendar day: somewhere to write “Bring passports, ferry leaves 7:40” against Day 3. Pinned vs. unpinned ideas: an idea with an exact map spot and one without (a Viator tour, “find a good taco place”) look almost identical today. The only difference is an 8.5px label at the bottom of the card.

If I had to pick

A1 + A3 for notes: a note card under the day header on the Plan tab, a small dot on the day chips for days that have one, and the same notes shown in the “All days” list. B1 for ideas: a solid teal pin badge on pinned cards and a hollow “No spot” badge on the rest, with the same pin mark carried onto calendar blocks. It's the smallest change, and it works the same way on the board, the calendar and the Add list.

A · Notes on a day

Each note belongs to a day of the trip (Day 3, not Mar 14), so it moves with the plan the way places and plans already do when dates shift.

A1 · Note card under the day header recommended

A yellow card sits between the day's places line and the calendar. Tap it to type. A day without a note shows a quiet dashed “Add a note for Day 3” row, so you can see where notes go without it taking space.

  • Shows the first two lines, then “more”. A long note doesn't push the calendar off screen.
  • The day chips get a small yellow corner dot for days that have a note, so you can spot them from any day.
  • Everyone can read it. Planners and owners can edit it, same as places.
  • Shows who last edited it and when (“Sam · 2h ago”).

Try it: tap the note on the left phone to edit it. Tap the dashed row on the right phone.

Effort: small · one text field per trip day (trip_day_places already keys by day)

A2 · Notes on the calendar itself

A note can be for the whole day, shown in a strip at the top of the calendar, or pinned to a time. A note for a time is a small yellow tag in the calendar at that hour (“7:15 · be at the pier”), drawn over the blocks without moving them.

  • Good for reminders that belong to a moment: tickets to print before you leave, a reservation name at lunch.
  • Added from the existing “+ Add” sheet as a new choice, “Note”.
  • Downside: more to tap around on a busy day. Tags can cover the block under them on a narrow phone.
Effort: medium · notes with an optional start minute, laid out over the grid

A3 · Notes in “All days”, written from a sheet

The whole-trip “Where we'll be” list gets each day's note under its places, so you can read the trip's notes top to bottom. Tapping “Add a note” on any day opens a sheet. This works alongside A1 or A2 rather than replacing them: the Plan tab is where you see one day, this is where you see all of them.

  • Handy before the trip: read through every day's notes in one place.
  • Same sheet used from the Plan tab, so there's one way to write a note.

B · Pinned vs. unpinned ideas

“Pinned” means the idea has an exact spot (lat/lng). It shows as a dot on the Map tab and gets ride times on the plan. “Unpinned” ideas only know their region, so on the map they're a count in the region's area, and on the plan they get no ride.

B1 · A pin badge on every card recommended

The photo gets a badge in its top-left corner. Pinned ideas get a solid teal map pin. Unpinned ideas get a white badge with a crossed-out hollow pin and “No spot”. Your eye catches teal vs. white before reading anything.

  • Teal already means “on the map” in the app (the places line, the map dots), so it reads the same everywhere.
  • The same pin mark goes on calendar blocks and in the Add list, so you can tell which stops will get rides.
  • Pass badges move to the bottom-left of the photo to make room.
  • Tapping “No spot” opens “Find on Google” to give it a spot.
Effort: small · reuses pin.lat, no backend change

Same mark on the calendar

B2 · Split the board in two

A switch at the top of the board: “On the map” and “No spot”, each with a count. Unpinned ideas get a compact list with a “Find spot” shortcut, since they usually have no good photo anyway.

  • Clearest separation. You never see the two kinds mixed.
  • Makes “No spot” a to-do list: pin these before planning.
  • Downside: you can't see all ideas for a region in one place, and it adds a control to a screen that already has region chips.

Try it: switch between the two tabs.

Board with a switch

B3 · Two different card styles

Pinned ideas look like they do now, with a faint teal edge and teal region line. Unpinned ideas become dashed “ticket” cards: no photo area, a small initial in a square, a sunken background. The dashed edge matches how the plan review already draws a stop with no spot.

  • Very easy to tell apart, even when scrolling fast.
  • Unpinned cards are shorter, so the board packs tighter.
  • Downside: an unpinned idea with a nice photo (a Viator tour) loses it. It can also read as “unfinished” or less important, which may not be fair to a booked tour.
Effort: small to medium · second card variant
