Tickets, passes and rentals

Where a Universal 5-day ticket or a rental car goes. Short answer: not on the Ideas board. The board is for places the group might go, and it stays that way. These costs are added and kept in Expenses. The one place they touch the board is a badge on the parks a ticket covers. The add-a-cost sheet in section 2 works; tap it.

Section 1

The Ideas board stays places-only

Each card on the board is somewhere you could go: it has a region, a spot on the map, a duration and hearts. A rental car or a park ticket has none of that. The proposal is to send these costs to Expenses as you add them, and show tickets on the board only where they mean something: on the parks they get you into.

Today: the only way to price a rental car is as an idea (Pin) with cost_per = "day". It goes through New idea, takes a region and a duration, and appears on the board, the map and the Plan tray. A ticket paid once isn't counted at all until someone drags it onto the calendar.

Section 2

Adding a cost in Expenses

Expenses gets an Add a cost button. The sheet covers a rental, a ticket or pass, and anything else (parking, a resort fee, insurance). A cost can be paid once or per day, priced per person or for the group, and for everyone or just some of the travelers. The sheet below works: pick a type, change the price, the days or who's going, and the receipt updates.

Section 3

Other board options considered

Three other ways to show these costs on the Ideas board, and why the proposal doesn't use them.

A · Off the board, badge on covered parks proposed

What sections 1 and 2 show.

  • The board, map and Plan tray only show places, so nothing needs filtering out
  • The badge explains why a park shows no price
  • You can't heart Enterprise vs. Alamo. Write it in the cost's notes or a comment

B · A "Trip costs" strip above the cards

A thin row of cost cards at the top of the board, like the stays strip in the earlier stays mockup.

  • Everyone sees the big fixed costs while choosing places
  • It's still costs on a board of places, just in a separate row
  • Duplicates Expenses, so there are two places to edit the same cost

C · A "Getting around" type, filtered

What the earlier types mockup drew: the rental car as an idea with a type, hidden behind a filter.

  • No new screen
  • It's still on the map and the Plan tray, with a duration it doesn't have
  • Doesn't help with tickets: who it covers, or counting it twice
Decisions

What needs your call

Board and entry point

  1. Off the board with park badges (option A)? rec
  2. Show the "Add as a cost" row only when the search text looks like a cost, or always at the top?
  3. Should a ticket's badge also appear on the Map's park markers?

Money rules

  1. A ticket paid once: show it in full on its first day rec, or spread across the days it's valid?
  2. "Who's it for" on stays too (a second room for part of the group)?
  3. Ship Covers these ideas with the rest, or first as a hint only ("this park has its own price")?

Underneath

  • Pins get kind = "expense", plus expense_type and traveler_ids (null means everyone).
  • Covered ideas: covers_pin_ids on the ticket. The park's price is skipped for the ticket holders.
  • Ideas board, Map, Plan tray, Google review and Compare filter out kind = "expense".
  • dailyCosts.js: counts a ticket paid once without it being on the calendar, and splits only among traveler_ids.
