Planning before the dates are set

You can start a trip as "about 5 days" and plan it by day number: Day 1, Day 2, and so on. When the real dates arrive, the days land on them in order. If a trip with real dates moves, one prompt asks whether the plan moves with it or stays on the calendar dates. To make that work, plans, stays and day trips are stored as a day of the trip instead of a calendar date.

Flow 1

A new trip with a length instead of dates

Step 1 of a new trip asks for a name and two dates. These screens add a "Not sure yet" choice that asks how long the trip is, and, if you like, roughly when. Everything after that is planned on numbered days.

Today: NewTrip.jsx step 1 has two optional date fields. Leave them empty and the trip says "Dates TBD", but nothing can go on the calendar. day_places.py refuses a trip with no start_date, and plans need a real starts_at.

Questions for you

  1. One number or a range? "About 5 days" or "4 to 6 days". A range doesn't change how you plan, since you'd still plan 6 days and leave one loose. Rec: one number
  2. Keep "Roughly when?" It's only a label for now. Later it could feed seasonal hints such as "cherry blossoms in late March". Rec: keep, optional
  3. Weekday rules on a trip without dates. If an idea says "closed Mondays", we can't tell which day is a Monday. Ignore the rule until there are dates, or let you pick a weekday for Day 1 without picking a date? Rec: ignore until dates

What stays the same

  • The ideas board, map and expenses don't depend on dates and don't change.
  • Pin availability (availability_rules.days) already uses trip-day numbers, so it works on a trip without dates as it is.
  • A rental car's days ("pick up Day 2, drop off Day 4") take day numbers too, so per-day costs add up before there are dates.
Flow 2

Changing the dates of a trip that's already planned

Trip settings keeps the toggle from 1a. When you save, the app asks a question only when the answer changes something: the start date moved and there are things on the calendar. The sample trip is Kyoto, Fri Mar 12 – Tue Mar 16, with 14 plans and four nights booked.

Today: stays and day trips (trip_day_places.date) and plans (plans.starts_at) stay on their calendar dates when the trip moves. Anything that ends up outside the trip's new dates is kept but hidden, and nothing tells you. The second option in 2b is today's behavior. The first one is new.
ChangeWhat happens
No dates → datesNo prompt. Day 1 lands on the start date and the other days follow in order (2a).
Start date movesPrompt: shift everything with the trip, or keep things on their dates (2b, 2c).
Only the end date moves, or the length changesNo prompt, because Day 1 doesn't move and both answers would be the same. If days with plans are cut off, a warning shows before Save.
Dates → "Not sure yet"No prompt. Day numbers stay, the dates are forgotten, and the length is kept.
Nothing on the calendar yetNo prompt in any case.

  2. Things booked for a fixed date. A flight or a prepaid hotel shouldn't shift with the rest. The app can't tell those apart yet. Move everything together for now, and later add a "Booked for this date" lock that keeps a plan where it is? Rec: lock later

Other people on the trip

  • Only owners and planners can change dates, which is the same as today.
  • Everyone else gets a note on the trip: "Amanda moved Kyoto to Mar 19–23. The plan moved with it."
  • Open votes keep their options. The hours they're voting on move with the plan, or stay on their dates, along with everything else.
Under the hood

Storing days, not dates

Everything on the calendar gets a day of the trip (Day 1 = 0) and a time in minutes, instead of a timestamp. The trip has either real dates or only a length. "Shift" then changes nothing but the trip row. "Keep dates" subtracts the number of days Day 1 moved from every row, in one transaction.

Schema

  • trips: add length_days and rough_month (both nullable). Either start_date + end_date are set, or length_days is, enforced by a check constraint.
  • trip_day_places.date → day (int). The unique constraints move to (trip_id, day, …).
  • plans, contests, splits: starts_at/ends_at → start_min/end_min, counted in minutes from 00:00 on Day 1. Overlap checks stay simple integer comparisons. plan_items already count from the plan's start.
  • pins.cost_start_date/cost_end_date → cost_start_day/cost_end_day.
  • availability_rules/overrides already use day numbers and don't change.

API and migration

  • PATCH /trips/{id} takes move: "shift" | "keep_dates". If the start date changes while something is on the calendar and move is missing, it returns 409, so an older client can't make the choice silently.
  • The Alembic migration computes day = date − trip.start_date and minutes from the stored timestamp in the trip's local time. Rows that are already outside the trip get days below 0 or past the end. These are exactly the "set aside" items.
  • Responses can still include derived starts_at while the frontend moves over, so screens can switch one at a time.
  • Plans no longer depend on time zones: Day 3 at 09:00 is 09:00 wherever the trip is.
