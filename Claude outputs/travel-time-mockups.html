Travel time, on the days it happens

Worked through one real trip: you start in Houston, fly IAH to MCO, pick up a car and drive to the hotel. Feature 2 adds a longer trip: Hawaii, then Japan, then Hawaii again. Travel counts as planned time, because it takes up the day either way. You can add travel with just how long it takes. You don't need a place at either end the way Map routes do. And the flight's cost lands in Expenses without pretending to be a pin.

Feature 1

Add just travel time

A new Travel option in the day's + Add sheet. It needs one thing: how long. A mode, a from and to, and a price are all optional. When both ends are places the map knows, it offers Google's estimate. When they aren't, you type the minutes.

Today: travel comes in two ways, and both get in the way. A Custom event takes a title, a kind (Travel / Lodging / Other), minutes and a cost, so a flight is "Other, 150 minutes" with no from, to or times. A Map route (Plan a trip) needs a stop at each end, so you can't add "drive from the airport" unless the airport is an idea and is already on the day.

  1. Its own row in + Add, as a travel item with a mode. Recommend One form whether you start from the sheet, a gap chip, or Where we'll be (feature 2).

Open questions for you

  • Do you want Ferry and Boat as modes, or is Other enough?
  • Should a gap chip also show when there's no gap but the two places are far apart? That would flag "no time to get there" instead of offering travel.
  • Should flights take any time zone, or only "home" and "trip"? Two zones covers your trip. A trip with a stop in a third country needs any zone.
Feature 2

Long legs live on the days

There's no special "getting there" setting. A flight is a travel item from feature 1, placed on the day it leaves. That covers a trip with one flight each way and a trip with five. The first and last legs are just legs whose far end is home. Where we'll be shows each leg between the stays it connects, so the whole route reads top to bottom.

The example: fly Houston to Honolulu and stay 3 nights. Fly to Tokyo for 4 nights, then back to Honolulu for 2, then home. Going west, the flight to Tokyo crosses the date line and lands the next day. Coming back, the flight from Tokyo lands earlier on the same date, so Saturday happens twice.

Legs come from the travel items on the calendar, so this list never disagrees with the Plan tab. A change of place with no leg in between shows a dashed + travel prompt. It opens the 1b form with the from, to and day filled in.

Where a day's clock comes from

  1. The place you wake up, or the place you land on a day that starts in the air. Recommend Each day has one clock, set by its stay in Where we'll be. A flight's two ends are the only place that shows two time zones.
  2. One clock for the whole trip. Simple, but on a Japan leg every time would be 19 hours off, which defeats the calendar.

Open questions for you

  • When you add a flight to Tokyo, should Where we'll be fill in Tokyo for the days after it until the next leg? Or suggest it and let you confirm?
  • Is a day that exists twice (Sat 14 in 2d) better as two trip days, as shown? The other option is one Sat 14 with a Tokyo half and a Honolulu half.
  • The flight home lands Tue 17 at 1:40 AM, after the trip's last day. Should it end at midnight on Day 12 with "lands Tue 1:40 AM" as a note (shown), or should the trip grow a Day 13?
  • Group trips: is it enough for each flight to have its own Who's going, so one friend can fly in from Denver on a different flight? Or does that need more than a block per traveler?
Feature 3

Day 1 on the Plan tab

Travel blocks are real blocks. They count as planned time and you can't place over them. They're teal so you can spot them at a glance. Booked ones (the flights) carry a lock and can't be dragged. The drive can be dragged like anything else.

Feature 4

Expenses

Travel is priced like everything else: per person or for the group, and split among whoever's going. It shows up as soon as it's on the calendar. Flights and other long legs are grouped in their own card, because they're usually the biggest numbers and don't really belong to one day.

For later

What it would change underneath

A sketch only, to show the size of the work. Nothing here is decided.

Travel items

  • mode gains flight and other next to car / bus / train / walk.
  • from_label / to_label (text), plus optional from_pin_id / to_pin_id when an end is a place, so estimates and the next ride can start there.
  • booked: a fixed time that can't be dragged and asks before Move dates shifts it.
  • depart_tz / arrive_tz for flights only. Each day shows its own clock (see Days and clocks).

Days and clocks

  • Each trip day gets a time zone, taken from its stay's location (Google's Time Zone API, looked up once when the stay is set). A day that starts in the air takes the landing airport's zone.
  • Trip days are already indexed by day number, not by date, so a date that happens twice is two days with the same label.
  • Where we'll be reads legs from placed travel items with a mode and a from and to. Nothing is stored twice.

Money and planner

  • Expenses groups travel items whose mode is flight, train or bus into the Travel between places card. They still come from plans, as they do today.
  • The day metrics add a travel sum (items with a mode). Map trip planning can start from a landing's to_pin_id.
