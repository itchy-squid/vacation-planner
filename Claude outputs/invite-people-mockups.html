Mockups · Oct 5, 2026

Inviting your people

Two changes. You can invite people you've planned with to a trip that already exists, and pick who they are on the traveler list (for example, swap them in for "Jonah", whom you'd listed by name). When you start a new trip, past travelers who never joined the app, like Kai and Grandma Hua, can go on the list of who's going.

1 · Invite to an existing trip 2 · Past travelers on a new trip
Feature 1

Invite your people to a trip you've already made

Today, picking people you've planned with only happens on step 2 of a new trip. After that, the only way in is a link. These screens add the same people list to Trip settings and let each invite take over a traveler who's already listed.

What exists: GET /api/people, direct invites (trip_invites.invitee_email) that wait on the invitee's Trips screen, and per-traveler links (POST /travelers/{id}/invite) that sign the person in as that traveler. What's new: a direct invite can carry a traveler_id, so it does both. The person is invited by email and claims that row when they join.

Decisions to make

  1. When they join, whose name does the row show? Suggest Their account name. "Traveler 5" becomes "Priya Shah". The trip owner can rename it.
  2. Should a same-name match be picked for you? Suggest Yes, only when exactly one unclaimed traveler has the same first name. Otherwise default to "new traveler".
  3. Can planners send these, or only the owner? Suggest Same as the links today: owner only (trip:manage).

Build notes

  • POST /trips/{id}/invites/direct takes [{email, role, traveler_id? | new_traveler | not_going}]. It reuses the TripCreate.invitees path.
  • The traveler_id on trip_invites already exists for links. A direct invite sets it too, and accepting claims that row.
  • This also closes a known gap: the general link stops offering a traveler who has a direct invite waiting.
  • Extract WhoIsPlanning's list into one component that the new-trip step and the Invite sheet share.
Feature 2

Past travelers who aren't on the app, on a new trip

"Who's planning with you?" only lists people with accounts. Kai and Grandma Hua came on Taiwan without accounts, so you'd have to add them again by hand. Here they show up in the same list. Ticking one lists them as a traveler, and nobody gets an invite.

Where they come from: travelers with no contributor_id on trips you're a member of. Placeholders like "Traveler 5" are left out. GET /api/people returns them as entries with on_app: false, keyed by name and the person who paid for them, along with who paid last time.

Decisions to make

  1. How do we tell it's the same Kai across trips? Suggest Same name and same payer's email. Two Kais paid for by different people stay as two rows. No new identity table.
  2. Who sees these people? Suggest Anyone who shared the trip. Kai is listed on Mei's trip, so you see Kai too. The alternative is to show them only to the person who paid.
  3. Do they show on the People tab? Suggest Yes, in their own section without a role, read-only.
  4. Should the same list come to feature 1's Invite sheet, to add Kai to an existing trip? Suggest Yes. It costs nothing more once the list component is shared.

Build notes

  • PersonOut gains on_app, key (email, or name|payer-email) and last_paid_by. Pick state in NewTrip is keyed by key instead of email.
  • TripCreate gains listed: [{name, paid_by: "me" | email | null}]. Payers are resolved after the invitees' travelers exist.
  • A "Traveler N" placeholder from the headcount migration is never offered.
